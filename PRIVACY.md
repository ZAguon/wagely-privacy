# Wagely Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Wagely ("the app") is a time clock that helps you track hours and expected pay across your jobs. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Information we collect
We do not collect any personal information. Wagely has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

## Information stored on your device
The jobs, hourly rates, shifts, breaks and settings you enter are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Your data may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or a computer backup), according to your device settings.

## Exports
If you export a timesheet as CSV or PDF, the file is created on your device. Where it goes after that is up to you.

## Third parties
Wagely does not share data with third parties and does not use third-party services.

## Children
Wagely does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
